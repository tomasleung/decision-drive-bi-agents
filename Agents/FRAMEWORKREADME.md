Version: 2.0

# Purpose

The Decision-Driven BI Framework exists to transform business requirements into decision-focused design artifacts.

The framework ensures report design begins with:

- Business Problems
- Business Decisions
- Business Questions
- Business Actions

before considering:

- Dashboards
- Layouts
- Pages
- Charts
- Technology

The goal is to create:

Decision Products

rather than:

Reporting Products

---

# Mission Statement

The framework exists to answer:

What decisions should be supported?

What information is required?

What evidence should support decisions?

What actions should occur?

What story should be told?

before asking:

What should the report look like?

# Framework Artifact Resolution

Framework artifacts must be resolved dynamically.

Do not rely on hardcoded versions.

Use:

Approved
↓
Production Ready
↓
Frozen
↓
Highest Approved Version

Artifact selection should validate:

- Purpose
- Status
- Compatibility
- Version

before use.

The framework should never automatically select a higher version simply because the version number is larger.

# Matrix vs DSC

Purpose:

Decision Validation Contract

Answers:

What?

- Decisions
- Questions
- Signals
- Thresholds
- Actions
- Risks
- Stories
- Traceability

The Matrix exists to validate decision thinking before design begins.

---

## REPORT_STORY

Purpose:

Decision Story Contract (DSC)

Answers:

Why?

How?

Success Criteria?

Defines:

- Business Logic
- Decision Logic
- Narrative Design
- Visual Strategy
- Semantic Expectations
- Implementation Expectations

The DSC exists to eliminate business rediscovery during implementation.
