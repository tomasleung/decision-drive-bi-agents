# Decision-Driven BI Architecture v3.0

## Framework Constitution

Version: 3.0

Status: Approved

Maturity: Production Ready

---

# PURPOSE

Provide a governed, repeatable, AI-ready framework for designing, validating, modeling, building, and deploying decision-driven business intelligence solutions.

The framework separates:

Business Discovery
↓
Business Design
↓
Technical Design
↓
Semantic Design
↓
Semantic Build
↓
Report Build

so that every artifact:

- Has a single responsibility
- Answers a specific question
- Has a defined owner
- Has a defined approval gate
- Has a governed handoff

to the next phase.

---

# MISSION STATEMENT

The framework exists to transform:

Business Requirements

into

Decision Products

rather than

Reporting Products.

The framework ensures reporting begins with:

- Business Problems
- Business Decisions
- Business Questions
- Business Actions

before considering:

- Dashboards
- Visuals
- Pages
- Layouts
- Technologies

---

# FRAMEWORK PRINCIPLES

## Principle 01 — Decision First

Every artifact should support a business decision.

No decision

↓

No artifact.

---

## Principle 02 — Question Driven

Every decision should generate business questions.

Questions drive information requirements.

---

## Principle 03 — Signal Driven

Questions require evidence.

Signals provide evidence.

---

## Principle 04 — Action Oriented

Signals must support action.

Information without action has limited value.

---

## Principle 05 — Story Driven

Business users consume stories.

Not tables.

Not datasets.

Not technical models.

---

## Principle 06 — Governance First

Every artifact must be:

- Traceable
- Reviewable
- Governed
- Auditable
