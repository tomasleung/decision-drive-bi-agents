# Purpose

This folder contains the implementation guidelines used by the Decision Story Agent.

Guidelines define:

- How To Apply Standards
- How To Analyze Inputs
- How To Discover Business Problems
- How To Discover Business Capabilities
- How To Discover Business Outcomes
- How To Discover Decisions
- How To Discover Questions
- How To Discover Signals
- How To Discover Actions
- How To Design Stories
- How To Validate Outputs
- How To Assess Readiness
- How To Prepare Handoffs

The guidelines ensure Decision Story generation remains:

- Consistent
- Repeatable
- Traceable
- Outcome Driven
- Decision Driven
- Action Oriented
- Human Governed
- Promotion Ready

across all projects.

---

# Why Guidelines Exist

Standards define:

What Good Looks Like

Guidelines define:

How To Achieve It

Without guidelines, different analysts, developers, and AI models may interpret the same business requirements differently.

Guidelines provide a repeatable methodology for transforming:

Business Requirements
↓
Business Context
↓
Business Outcomes
↓
Decision Framework
↓
Story Framework
↓
Decision Story Contract

into a governed business design.

# Human Authority Principle

The framework is:

AI Assisted
↓
Human Governed

AI may:

- Discover
- Analyze
- Recommend
- Structure

AI may not:

- Own Decisions
- Approve Outcomes
- Assume Accountability

Final authority remains with business stakeholders.
