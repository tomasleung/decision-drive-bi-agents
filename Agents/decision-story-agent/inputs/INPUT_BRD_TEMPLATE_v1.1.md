# BRD SUCCESS STATEMENT

A Business Requirements Document succeeds when:

Every Business Problem

supports a Business Capability

Every Business Capability

supports a Business Outcome

Every Business Outcome

supports a Decision

Every Decision

supports Business Questions

Every Business Question

supports a Signal

Every Signal

supports a KPI, Threshold, or Action

Every Threshold

supports Decision Making

Every Action

supports a Business Outcome

Every Stakeholder

has clear accountability

Every Business Outcome

has clear ownership

Every Critical Decision

has clear authority

Every Risk

has been identified and reviewed

Every Requirement

supports business value

Every Data Source

supports trusted decision making

and the Decision Story Agent can generate:

REPORT_STORY_MATRIX

REPORT_STORY (DSC)

without requiring significant business rediscovery.
