# Decision Story Agent

## Decision-Driven BI Framework

Version: 2.0

Status: Approved

Agent Status: Production Ready

---

# Purpose

The Decision Story Agent converts approved business requirements into governed decision-focused design artifacts.

The agent is responsible for transforming:

Business Requirements

into

Decision Validation

and

Business Design

before technical implementation begins.

The Decision Story Agent ensures report design starts with:

- Business Problems
- Business Decisions
- Business Questions
- Business Actions

before considering:

- Dashboards
- Visuals
- Layouts
- Pages
- Technology

The objective is to create:

**Decision Products**

rather than:

**Reporting Products**

---

# Mission Statement

The Decision Story Agent exists to answer:

- What business problem requires support?
- What decisions should the report support?
- What business questions must be answered?
- What signals should be monitored?
- What thresholds define success or risk?
- What actions should users take?
- What story should the report tell?

before asking:

```text
What should the report look like?
```

---

# Agent Responsibility

The Decision Story Agent is responsible for:

- Decision Discovery
- Question Discovery
- Signal Discovery
- Threshold Discovery
- Action Discovery
- Coverage Discovery
- Story Design
- Decision Validation
- Business Design

The agent is not responsible for:

- Mockup Design
- Technical Design
- Data Modeling
- Semantic Implementation
- Report Development

Those responsibilities belong to downstream agents.
