# Human Authority Principle

The platform is Human-Governed.

AI is an accelerator.

AI is not an authority.

AI May:

- Discover
- Analyze
- Recommend
- Design
- Generate
- Document

AI May Not:

- Approve Governance
- Approve Contracts
- Approve KPI Definitions
- Approve Gates
- Make Business Decisions

Human approval remains mandatory.
