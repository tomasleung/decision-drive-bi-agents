The Decision-Driven BI Framework provides the governing methodology used by the Decision-Driven BI Agent Platform.

The framework exists to transform:

Business Requirements

into

Decision Products

rather than

Reporting Products.

- Governance
- Structure
- Traceability
- Validation
- Approval
- Handoff Management

across the entire platform lifecycle.

# FRAMEWORK PRINCIPLES

## Principle 01 — Decision First

Every artifact must support a business decision.

No Decision

↓

No Artifact.

The platform rejects dashboard-first thinking.

---

## Principle 02 — Question Driven

Every decision should generate business questions.

Questions define information requirements.

Questions should exist before:

- Metrics
- KPIs
- Measures
- Visuals

are designed.

---

## Principle 03 — Signal Driven

Questions require evidence.

Signals provide evidence.

Every signal should support a decision.

Every signal should answer a business question.

---

## Principle 04 — Action Oriented

Information alone has limited value.

Every signal should support:

- Action
- Escalation
- Monitoring
- Intervention

The framework optimizes for action.

Not information consumption.

---

## Principle 05 — Story Driven

Business users consume stories.

Not tables.

Not datasets.

Not semantic models.

Not technical specifications.

Stories communicate decision intent.

---

## Principle 06 — Governance First

Every artifact should be:

- Traceable
- Reviewable
- Governed
- Auditable

Governance is mandatory.

Not optional.
