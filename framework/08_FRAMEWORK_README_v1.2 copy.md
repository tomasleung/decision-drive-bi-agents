- What business outcome improves?
- What business risk is reduced?

- Agent Level

# FRAMEWORK PRINCIPLES

## Principle 01 — Decision First

Every artifact must support a business decision.

No Decision

↓

No Artifact.

The platform rejects:

- Dashboard First Thinking
- KPI First Thinking
- Report First Thinking

Decision context must exist before solution design begins.

---

## Principle 02 — Question Driven

Every decision should generate business questions.

Questions define information requirements.

Questions should exist before:

- Metrics
- KPIs
- Measures
- Visuals

are designed.

---

## Principle 03 — Signal Driven

Questions require evidence.

Signals provide evidence.

Every signal should:

- Support a Decision
- Support a Question
- Support an Action

Signals should not exist without decision relevance.

---

## Principle 04 — Action Oriented

Information alone has limited value.

Every signal should support:

- Action
- Escalation
- Monitoring
- Intervention

The framework optimizes for:

Business Action

not

Information Consumption.

---

## Principle 05 — Story Driven

Business users consume stories.

Not tables.

Not datasets.

Not semantic models.

Not technical specifications.

Stories communicate decision intent.

Stories organize information into decision journeys.

---

## Principle 06 — Governance First

Every artifact should be:

- Traceable
- Reviewable
- Governed
- Auditable

Governance is mandatory.

Not optional.

---

## Principle 07 — Human In The Loop

AI accelerates delivery.

Humans govern outcomes.

Governance authority always remains with human stakeholders.

All critical decisions require human ownership and approval.

---

## Principle 08 — Minimal Architecture

The platform should use the minimum complexity required to support the decision.

Prefer:

- Simplicity
- Maintainability
- Transparency

over:

- Unnecessary Complexity
- Overengineering
- Technology-Driven Design

Architecture should exist to support decisions.

Not technology preferences.
